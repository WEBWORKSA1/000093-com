About 000093

An independent publisher of free tools and plain-English guides to China's numbers.

Our mission

More than a billion people use numbers as a second language: six-digit stock codes, lucky phone numbers, auspicious dates, numeric web addresses. Outsiders mostly see noise. We make these numbers readable for investors, founders, students and anyone curious.

What we do

  • Tools: a securities-code decoder, a lucky-number analyzer and quizzes.
  • Guides: explainers on China's markets, trading rules and access routes.
  • Culture: the history and symbolism behind numbers such as 九三.
  • Connections: we route business requests to vetted specialists.

Editorial standards

We cite primary sources where possible and correct errors quickly. We label all advertising and sponsorship. Sponsors have no say over editorial content. Nothing here is investment, legal or tax advice.

Independence

The name is a number. 000093.com has no affiliation with any exchange, fund, listed company, political organisation or other entity that uses or is associated with "000093", "00093" or "九三".
