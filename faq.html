Frequently asked questions

Can't find your answer? Contact us.

Is 000093.com affiliated with any stock, fund or organisation?
No. We're an independent educational publisher. The domain is a number, and we have no connection to any exchange, issuer, fund manager or organisation that uses it.
Are the tools free?
Yes. They're free, you don't need to sign up, and they run in your browser. We're funded by ads, sponsors, consulting referrals and reader support.
How accurate is the code decoder?
It follows the published prefix conventions of each exchange. Code ranges can change and some number spaces overlap, so always check the official exchange site before you act.
Is the lucky-number score scientific?
No. It's a cultural reading based on common Chinese homophones and combinations, for entertainment and education.
Is this investment advice?
No. Nothing on this site is investment, legal or tax advice.
How do donations work?
Choose an amount on the Support page and pay through a listed provider, or send a pledge and we'll reply with a secure payment link.
How are contest winners picked?
A panel scores the entries against published criteria. Winners are notified by email and shown in the public gallery. See the rules.
Can I advertise or buy this domain?
Yes. See Advertise & Partner or go to web.works/contact.
