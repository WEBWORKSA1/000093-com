Frequently asked

Why can the same six digits mean two different things?
China uses more than one numbering system. Exchange listings, index codes and off-exchange mutual-fund codes can overlap. 000001 is a Shenzhen bank stock on SZSE and also the SSE Composite Index code. Always check the market suffix (.SH / .SZ / .BJ / .HK).
Can foreigners buy A-shares?
Yes, through Northbound Stock Connect via a Hong Kong or international broker, through QFII/RQFII, or through ETFs that track Chinese indices. STAR and ChiNext names in Stock Connect are generally limited to institutional/professional investors.
What do the suffixes .SS, .SZ and .HK mean?
These are data-vendor suffixes: .SS or .SH for Shanghai, .SZ for Shenzhen, .BJ for Beijing and .HK for Hong Kong. They tell you which exchange a code belongs to.
