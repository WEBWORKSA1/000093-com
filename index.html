What does "000093" mean?

  • Market code: the 000 prefix is Shenzhen Main Board territory. The same digits also appear in fund and index numbering.
  • Culture: 九三 (9·3) is 3 September, China's Victory Day in the War of Resistance (1945).
  • Numerology: 9 = 久, "lasting". 3 = 生, "life/growth". Four zeros suggest a clean slate.
