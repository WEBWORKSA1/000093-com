# 000093 — Research Brief & Business Case

## 1. What "000093" means in China

| Lens | Finding | Commercial implication |
|---|---|---|
| **Securities code format** | Mainland China identifies every stock, fund, bond and index with a **6-digit code**. The `000` prefix is **Shenzhen Main Board** territory. The `000xxx` space is also used for **Shanghai-side index codes** (000001 SSE Composite, 000300 CSI 300) and many **off-exchange mutual-fund codes**. | The domain *looks like* a market code. Hundreds of millions of Chinese retail investors read that format every day. Finance is among the highest-CPC AdSense verticals. |
| **Specific historical use** | Fund code **000093** was a tiered bond-fund "B" share class launched **9 May 2013** (Eastmoney archive; tiny remaining AUM by 2020). In Hong Kong's 5-digit system, **00093** is a Main Board lending/fintech listing. | Informational search demand exists for the code. **We must not brand around either entity** (trademark risk), so the site is positioned as an *independent decoder*. |
| **History — 九三 (9·3)** | 3 September 1945 = China's **Victory Day** in the War of Resistance against Japan (made an official national commemoration day in 2014; big anniversary events in 2015 and 2025). A scholarly organisation founded in 1946 took its name from the date. | A predictable **annual traffic spike every September**, plus evergreen history content. We avoid political branding and use neutral, educational framing. |
| **Numerology** | 9 = 久 "long-lasting"; 3 = 生 "life/growth" (also 散 "scatter"); 0 = wholeness. **93 → 久生, "lasting life"**. Neutral-to-positive. | Supports a **lucky-number analyzer**, a high-traffic, shareable tool with viral loops (phone numbers, plates, dates, domains). |
| **Numeric-domain culture** | Chinese users prefer digits over pinyin. Numeric domains are an asset class (e.g. 55.com ≈ $2.3M in 2011; 2015 China-driven buying wave). "CHIP" names (no 0 or 4) command premiums. | Domain-brokerage leads and valuation requests fit naturally. Honest note: 000093 contains 0s, so its value comes from the **"stock-code" look**, not CHIP status. |

## 2. Chosen concept — "000093: China's Numbers, Decoded"

An English-first, bilingual-ready **utility + education hub** with three traffic engines and five revenue lines.

**Traffic engines**
1. **Stock-Code Decoder** (evergreen, high-intent finance searches: "what does 600 mean", "688 stock code", "000001").
2. **Lucky Number Analyzer + Quiz** (viral, social, repeat use).
3. **Insights & 九三 culture hub** (SEO long-tail + a seasonal September spike).

**Revenue lines (ranked by expected $/visit)**
1. **Lead generation.** China market-entry, investment-research, sourcing and numeric-domain requests via a 3-step form. B2B leads are typically worth far more than ad impressions.
2. **AdSense.** Finance/investing content attracts premium advertisers. Labelled slots are placed away from tool inputs.
3. **Sponsorships.** "Decoder powered by…" tool sponsorships, newsletter placements, contest prize funding.
4. **YouTube.** Explainer videos embedded site-wide; channel revenue plus sponsored integrations.
5. **Reader support.** One-time and monthly donations, a supporter wall and a progress bar.

**Why this beats the alternatives**
- A pure *fund/stock data site* needs licensed real-time data (costly and legally risky) and competes with Eastmoney and Sina.
- A pure *lucky-number site* monetises poorly (low CPC).
- The **hybrid** combines viral tools (traffic) with finance and B2B intent (CPC and leads) on a free static stack (zero hosting cost).

## 3. Reference sites surveyed (34 fetched; 29 usable, 2 partial, 3 failed)
Features were extracted from: investing.com, tradingview.com, finance.yahoo.com, morningstar.com, stockanalysis.com, finviz.com, macrotrends.net, nerdwallet.com, bankrate.com, calculator.net, seekingalpha.com, china-briefing.com, scmp.com, sixthtone.com, caixinglobal.com, chinahighlights.com, yourchineseastrology.com, travelchinaguide.com, chinadaily.com.cn, ltl-school.com, hkex.com.hk, szse.cn, buymeacoffee.com, ko-fi.com, gofundme.com, gleam.io, wellfound.com, hubspot.com, unbounce.com, afternic.com, devpost.com (partial). investopedia.com was blocked, and kaggle/devpost listings were rendered by JavaScript.

Key patterns adopted:
- A search box that accepts any code, with trending chips (StockAnalysis/HKEX).
- A one-line explainer above every tool (Macrotrends).
- Star/score cards (yourchineseastrology).
- A numeral table with 汉字/pinyin (LTL).
- A two/three-step lead form with trust badges and a free lead magnet (HubSpot/Unbounce/China Briefing).
- Preset amounts, a one-time/monthly toggle, a supporter wall and a progress bar (BMC/Ko-fi/GoFundMe).
- Contests with a countdown, bonus referral entries, public rules, a named judging method and fraud controls (Gleam).
- Job cards with type filters and a contributor route (Wellfound/Seeking Alpha).
- Ads labelled "Advertisement/Sponsored" and kept out of tool inputs (SCMP/Caixin/Yahoo).
- Light/dark mode and an EN/中文 toggle (Finviz/China Daily).
- FAQ schema and disclaimers (Bankrate/NerdWallet).
