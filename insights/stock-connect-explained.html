Stock Connect, explained

By the 000093 Desk6-min read

Stock Connect is the cross-border link between Hong Kong and the Shanghai and Shenzhen exchanges. Shanghai Connect launched in 2014 and Shenzhen Connect in 2016. It's the main way international investors trade mainland A-shares without an onshore licence.

Two directions

  • Northbound: international investors, through Hong Kong brokers, buy eligible Shanghai and Shenzhen shares. Flows are widely watched as "北向资金".
  • Southbound: eligible mainland investors buy eligible Hong Kong shares.

What's eligible?

Mostly constituents of major indices plus A-shares with H-share listings. The eligible universe expands from time to time, and it's published by the exchanges.

Mechanics to know

  • A-share rules apply: T+1 for shares, daily price limits, 100-share lots.
  • Trading happens only when both markets are open, so check holiday calendars.
  • Daily quotas cap net buying in each direction.
  • Shares are held through Hong Kong's central clearing system (nominee structure).

Risks

Currency (CNY), regulatory and policy shifts, liquidity in small caps, and suspensions. This is education, not advice.
