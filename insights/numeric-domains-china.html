Numeric domains and the Chinese internet

By the 000093 Desk6-min read

Chinese characters can't be typed directly into a classic domain name, and pinyin spellings are easy to get wrong. Digits are universal. That's why some of China's best-known web properties used numeric addresses and why numeric .coms became an asset class of their own.

The scarcity math

LengthPossible .coms
2 digits (NN)100
3 digits (NNN)1,000
4 digits (NNNN)10,000
5 digits100,000
6 digits1,000,000

Scarcity drives price. Two- and three-digit .coms have sold for six and seven figures. In 2015, a wave of investment from China quickly cleared out the remaining short numeric names.

"CHIP" names

Chinese investors often prefer "Chinese premium" names that contain no 0 or 4. Names with a leading 0 are valued less by that group. But a six-digit name starting with 000 reads exactly like a mainland securities code, which is a natural fit for finance, data and market-education brands.

What drives value

  • Length: shorter is rarer.
  • Lucky digits (8, 6, 9) and patterns (888, 168, 518).
  • Repetition and memorability (AAAA, ABAB).
  • A readable meaning (dates, codes, slang).
  • Traffic, history and a clean trademark position.
