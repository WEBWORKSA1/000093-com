STAR Market vs ChiNext

By the 000093 Desk5-min read

China runs two flagship growth boards, one in each of its original exchange cities. They look alike on a quote screen: both have a ±20% daily band and both use registration-based IPOs. But they aim at different companies.

How to tell them apart fast

Look at the code. 688 means STAR and 300 means ChiNext. Then check the sector. STAR companies often report heavy R&D spending and may list before they turn a profit.
